## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand

**Winter severity:** None
**Annual snowfall:** < 1" (rare freeze events)
**AWD/4WD demand:** Low for crossovers. High for trucks (4WD for work/recreation, not winter)
**AWD premium over FWD (crossovers):** +0% — buyers don't pay for AWD in no-winter market
**4WD premium (trucks/SUVs for work/towing):** +3% — utility-driven, not weather-driven
**Seasonal demand pattern:** Year-round; slight spike August–October (hurricane season trade-ins)

## 3. Buyer demographic profile

**Median household income:** ~$58,000–$64,000
**Median age:** 34
**Avg household size:** 2.8
**Dominant buyer types:**
- Oil/gas workers: buy trucks as tools, pay for towing and work packages
- Suburban families: mid-size SUVs, 3-row demand, value-conscious
- Hispanic community (largest in US): strong brand loyalty to Toyota, Honda, Chevrolet
- Construction/trades: full-size trucks, work packages, durability priority

**Price sensitivity:** Medium-High — payment-driven, value important
**Luxury market depth:** Moderate — Galleria / Memorial area has real money
**Payment vs. price:** Strongly payment-driven

## 7. Financing landscape

**Dominant lenders:** Lone Star Credit Union, RBFCU, Space City Credit Union, Chase, Wells Fargo
**Average used car loan rate:** 7–8.5% (April 2026)
**European brand financing:** Moderate friction — credit unions approve but prefer domestic
**Price ceiling for easy financing:** ~$50,000
**BHPH market:** Significant — large subprime population; BHPH lots strong in East Houston

## 8. Auction intelligence

**Primary auction:** Manheim Texas Hobby (Houston), ADESA Houston
**Competition:** Heavy — largest truck buyer concentration in US. Expect aggressive bidding on clean F-150s and Silverados.
**Transport to lot:** Minimal — most auction units are local

---

## 9. Local market signals

- Trucks are not a preference here — they are a necessity. F-150 / Silverado demand is structural.
- Hurricane season (June–November) creates trade-in spikes as owners upgrade before storm season.
- Oil price sensitivity: when oil is high, trucks and full-size SUVs spike. Watch WTI.
- Large Hispanic community: Toyota Camry, Honda CR-V, Chevrolet Silverado are cultural staples.
- No emission checks in most Texas counties — can retail higher-mileage units that wouldn't pass in CA or CO.
