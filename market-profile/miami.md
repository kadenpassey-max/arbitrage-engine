## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand
**Winter severity:** None
**AWD/4WD demand:** Very Low — buyers actively choose FWD/RWD
**AWD crossover premium:** −2% (FWD preferred; AWD seen as unnecessary cost)
**Seasonal demand:** Peaks October–March (snowbird/tourist season), slow June–September

**Avoid:** AWD-premium units, rust-prone vehicles from Northern states (buyers are savvy)

## 3. Buyer demographic
**Median HHI:** ~$58,000 (city) — wide spread; Brickell/Coral Gables much higher
**Median age:** 40
**Key buyers:** Latin American wealth (cash buyers, want luxury), young professionals,
  retirees, international buyers seeking US vehicles
**Luxury depth:** Deep — strongest European luxury market in Southeast
**Payment vs. price:** Mix — cash buyers for luxury, payment-driven for mid-market

## 6. Color / Financing / Auction
Color: White −3d (dominant preference), Silver −2d, Black +1d (heat aversion similar to Phoenix),
Red −1d (sports cars — fast-moving in Miami). Financing: Ocean Bank, City National, BankUnited.
Rate 7.5–9%. European financing easier here than most markets (international cash buyers).
Nearest auction: Manheim Florida, ADESA South Florida.
