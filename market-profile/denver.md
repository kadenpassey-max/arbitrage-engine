```
Profile tier: RESEARCHED
Research date: April 2026
Data sources: usedcars.com state trends (CO leads 4x4 searches at 35%), DealerClick

Notes: Most similar to SLC of all profiles. Mountain access, outdoor lifestyle, strong
```

## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand
**Winter severity:** Moderate–Severe (Front Range gets real winters; mountains more severe)
**Annual snowfall:** 57" Denver (similar to SLC), 300"+ in mountain corridors
**AWD/4WD demand:** Very High — Colorado leads the nation at 35% of searches
**AWD crossover premium:** +5% MMR (higher than SLC — Colorado AWD demand is strongest in US)
**4WD truck/Wrangler premium:** +5% MMR
**Seasonal demand:** Year-round; spring ski-season transition (April–May) is peak

## 3. Buyer demographic
**Median HHI:** ~$72,000 | **Median age:** 36 | **Avg HH size:** 2.5
**Key buyers:** Outdoor enthusiasts (skiing, hiking, climbing), tech workers (Boulder corridor),
  young professionals, families (smaller avg size than SLC)
**Luxury depth:** Moderate — Cherry Creek / LoDo has real money
