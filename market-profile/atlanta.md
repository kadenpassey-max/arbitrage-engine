## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand
**Winter severity:** Mild (rare ice events — 2014/2021 notable; not annual)
**AWD/4WD demand:** Low-Moderate (buyers want AWD option but don't require it)
**AWD crossover premium:** +2% MMR (modest — psychological more than practical)
**4WD truck premium:** +3%
**Seasonal demand:** Year-round; tax season (Feb–March) is strong peak
