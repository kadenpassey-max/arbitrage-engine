## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand
**Winter severity:** Mild (rain, not snow — Seattle proper rarely gets snow)
**AWD/4WD demand:** Moderate-High for different reasons than Mountain West — wet road traction
**AWD crossover premium:** +3% MMR (wet roads, not snow — still real but less than SLC/Denver)
**4WD truck premium:** +2%
**Seasonal demand:** Slight summer peak (weather improves; outdoor buying mood)
