## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand
**Winter severity:** Mild (rain-dominant; Cascade passes get snow — buyers notice)
**AWD demand:** Moderate-High (same rain-driven logic as Seattle)
**AWD premium:** +3% MMR
**Seasonal demand:** Summer peak; slowest November–January

## 3. Buyer demographic
**Median HHI:** ~$78,000 | **Median age:** 37 | **Avg HH size:** 2.3
**Key buyers:** Eco-conscious professionals, outdoor enthusiasts, creative industry
**Luxury depth:** Thin — Portland wage ceiling lower than Seattle
**Feature priority:** Efficiency, AWD, tech features, smaller footprint
