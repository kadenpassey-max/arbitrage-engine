## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand
**Winter severity:** None
**AWD/4WD demand:** Very Low for crossovers. Low for trucks (recreation, not work/weather).
**AWD crossover premium:** −1% (buyers actively prefer FWD for heat management and cost)
**4WD truck premium:** +1% (minimal — mostly off-road recreation use)
**Seasonal demand:** Slight spring/fall peak (snowbirds arrive; summer is slow)

**Key buyers:** Retirees/snowbirds (Scottsdale), young families (growing suburbs), tech workers
**Luxury depth:** Moderate — Scottsdale corridor has deep luxury buyer pool
