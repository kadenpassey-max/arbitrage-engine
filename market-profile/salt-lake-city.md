## 1. Climate and drivetrain demand

**Winter severity:** Moderate–Severe (valley) / Severe (mountain corridors)
**Annual snowfall:** 55–60" (Salt Lake Valley), 150"+ in canyons
**AWD/4WD demand:** Very High
**AWD premium over FWD:** +4% MMR
**4WD-specific premium (trucks, Wrangler):** +4% MMR (same — buyers treat equally)
**Seasonal demand pattern:** Year-round with slight spring peak (March–May)

## 3. Buyer demographic profile

**Median household income:** ~$65,000–$72,000
**Median age:** 31 (youngest major metro in US)
**Avg household size:** 3.1 (largest in US — LDS community)
**Dominant buyer types:**
- Young families (LDS community): strong minivan + 3-row demand, value-conscious
- Outdoor lifestyle buyers: Wrangler, 4WD trucks, AWD crossovers premium
- Tech workers (Silicon Slopes — Lehi/Draper): pay for features, open to HMA/Euro
- Military (Hill Air Force Base): value-oriented, reliable brands preferred

**Price sensitivity:** Medium — will stretch for the right vehicle
**Luxury market depth:** Moderate — east bench (Holladay, Millcreek, Draper) has real money
**Payment vs. price:** Payment-driven for most; price-driven for cash buyers (larger % here)

## 7. Financing landscape

**Dominant lenders:** Mountain America CU, America First CU, UCCU, Zions Bank
**Average used car loan rate:** 6.5–7.5% (April 2026)
**European brand financing:** Moderate friction — credit unions approve but at +0.5–1.5% vs. domestic
**Price ceiling for easy financing:** ~$45,000 — above this, payment calculator becomes barrier
**Notable lenders:** Mountain America CU (SLC-dominant, approves HMA readily), America First CU
**BHPH market:** Minor — not a significant sub-prime market

## 9. Local market signals

- **LDS community effect:** Largest family sizes of any US metro drives minivan and
  3-row SUV demand well above national average. Market accordingly.
- **Wrangler lifestyle premium is real:** SLC Wrangler buyers will drive from St. George
  or Ogden for the right unit. Don't undervalue hard-top AWD Wranglers here.
- **Silicon Slopes tech corridor:** Lehi, Draper, South Jordan have a tech buyer cohort
  that pays for features — HMA Limited trims, navigation, Bose, heads-up display.
- **Hill AFB nearby:** Military buyers are value-oriented but reliable — they finance
  through USAA or Navy Fed at low rates and close quickly on the right vehicle.
- **Outdoor recreation premium:** Canyon access (Cottonwood Canyons, Park City) means
  4WD is truly used here — not just a perceived feature.
